## Getting Started

1. **Assess Your Current Level**: Take a practice test to understand your strengths and areas for improvement
2. **Create a Study Plan**: Allocate time for each topic based on your assessment
3. **Practice Regularly**: Consistent practice is key to success
4. **Join Study Groups**: Collaborate with peers for better learning
5. **Track Your Progress**: Keep a record of problems solved and concepts mastered

## Tips for Success

- Start early and maintain consistency
- Focus on understanding concepts rather than memorizing
- Practice coding problems daily
- Review and revise regularly
- Take mock interviews to build confidence
- Stay updated with industry trends
- Network with professionals in your target companies

Remember: Success in placements is a marathon, not a sprint. Stay focused, keep practicing, and believe in yourself!
